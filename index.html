I'm an IB Diploma student at Oakridge International School in Hyderabad. Most of what I do sits where hardware, AI and business meet: I run a GPU rental marketplace, I've interned on private AI infrastructure, and I've reached the WRO India National Championship in three seasons.

I've built multiple computers and enjoy it. Renting one of them out is what led to rentcompute. Outside of that, I've published a research paper with IEEE and written a book on AI for older adults.
